Privacy Policy for Ay

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ay (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information. Ay is designed with a local-first architecture: App content is generally kept on your device unless you choose a feature that communicates with an external destination or an Apple-managed service, including web browsing through Net, or you deliberately export, share, save, synchronize, use Apple Intelligence, or back up content elsewhere. When you browse the web, the websites and online services you contact necessarily receive network requests and may independently process data as explained in Section 2.27.

We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, third-party crash analytics, data brokers, or cross-app tracking for Ay. This does not mean that third-party websites opened in Net cannot display advertisements, use their own trackers, or collect information under their own policies.

Most App data is stored locally on your device or in local/shared containers used by Ay and its extensions. The App does not operate a custom backend server and does not provide a server-based account system. Net uses internet connections to load user-requested websites and web resources directly through Apple WebKit and related system networking; these are not connections to a developer-operated Ay backend. Some features interact with Apple system frameworks or Apple-managed services. Use of an Apple framework does not by itself mean that data leaves your device or becomes accessible to the developer. Where an Apple-managed service processes data, Apple’s applicable privacy disclosures, permissions, terms, and device settings apply. Data that you deliberately export, share, save, synchronize, or back up outside Ay may also be handled by the destination you select.

For clarity, this Policy distinguishes local/on-device processing from information transmitted to or retained by a developer-operated service. Ay does not operate a backend service that receives your ordinary App content.
